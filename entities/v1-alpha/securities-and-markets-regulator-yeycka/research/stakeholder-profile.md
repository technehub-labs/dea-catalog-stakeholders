## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Protect investors (the SEC's first statutory mission).
- Maintain fair, orderly, and efficient markets.
- Facilitate capital formation.
- Enforce the securities laws through examinations and civil enforcement actions.

## What the class does

- Adopts and maintains securities-law rules (including the insider-trading regime's disclosure obligations).
- Operates EDGAR, the public disclosure system for regulated filings.
- Examines regulated entities (registrants, advisers, broker-dealers) for compliance.
- Brings civil enforcement actions, and operates a whistleblower program that rewards high-quality tips.

Should the enterprise ever hold registrant status, the SEC's disclosure and insider-trading regimes would bind directly.
