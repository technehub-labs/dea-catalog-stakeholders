## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Reuse the published corpus: study, run, fork, and extend the repositories under Apache-2.0.
- Redistribute derived works in compliance with the license (retain NOTICE/attribution conditions).
- Feed improvements back: raise issues and pull requests against the public repositories.
- Hold the enterprise accountable to its own published standards (the corpus carries its conformance gates publicly).

## What the class does

- Clones and consumes the published repositories of the technehub-labs organization (the OpenDEA catalog fleet).
- Exercises Apache-2.0 licensee rights (use, modification, redistribution subject to NOTICE).
- Files issues and pull requests on the public repositories.
- Reads the published CATALOG.yaml indexes, CHANGELOG, and CR history of each catalog repo.
