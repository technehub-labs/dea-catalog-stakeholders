# TechNeHub Labs open-source community — stakeholder profile

> Defining the stakeholder: what the party is, its goals, what it does, and how
> it engages the enterprise. Per `docs/EVIDENCE-STANDARD.md`; the record's
> description remains the normative one-liner — this profile expands it.

## Definition

The open-source licensee community is the collective of parties who consume, reuse, and redistribute the TechNeHub Labs corpus under its Apache-2.0 license terms. It is a collective-capacity record: there is no single named counterparty; the community is constituted by the license instrument itself and evidenced by the organization's public hosting surfaces.

The record's normative description (verbatim): *The open-source community engaging TechNeHub Labs' published corpus: licensees and consumers of the Apache-2.0-licensed OpenDEA catalogs and frameworks. External to the enterprise and affected by its licensing and publication decisions; distinct from internal performers (Actors catalog boundary).*

## Mandate and goals

- Reuse the published corpus: study, run, fork, and extend the repositories under Apache-2.0.
- Redistribute derived works in compliance with the license (retain NOTICE/attribution conditions).
- Feed improvements back: raise issues and pull requests against the public repositories.
- Hold the enterprise accountable to its own published standards (the corpus carries its conformance gates publicly).

## What they do

- Clones and consumes the published repositories of the technehub-labs organization (the OpenDEA catalog fleet).
- Exercises Apache-2.0 licensee rights (use, modification, redistribution subject to NOTICE).
- Files issues and pull requests on the public repositories.
- Reads the published CATALOG.yaml indexes, CHANGELOG, and CR history of each catalog repo.

## Engagement with the enterprise

The enterprise publishes its entire catalog fleet to this community under Apache-2.0; the license and notice instruments (channel 1) are the operative engagement. The community's platform-relationship edges are deferred until C-001; the collective's evidence surfaces are the org, the repositories, and the public site.
