## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Protect natural persons' fundamental rights to personal-data protection across the EEA (GDPR's objective).
- Ensure consistent application of the GDPR across member states (the EDPB's coordination mandate).
- Advise on data-protection matters and promote public awareness (GDPR Art 57 duties of supervisory authorities).
- Handle complaints, conduct investigations, and impose corrective powers including administrative fines (GDPR Art 58).

## What the class does

- Supervise controllers and processors established or offering services in the EU/EEA (GDPR Art 3 territorial scope).
- Issue guidelines, recommendations, and best-practice opinions (EDPB; binding on authorities under GDPR Art 64/65 procedures).
- Operate the one-stop-shop cooperation and consistency mechanisms for cross-border processing.
- Investigate breaches, order remedies, and impose fines up to the GDPR's administrative-fine tiers.
