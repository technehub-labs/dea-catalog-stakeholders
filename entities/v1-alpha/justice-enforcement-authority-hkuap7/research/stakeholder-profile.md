## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Enforce federal law and defend the interests of the United States (the department's mission).
- In the DTSA capacity: provide a uniform federal remedy for trade-secret misappropriation, alongside state law.
- Deter and prosecute federal crimes, including economic espionage and criminal trade-secret theft (18 U.S.C. § 1832).

## What the class does

- Investigates and prosecutes federal crimes (including economic espionage and trade-secret theft).
- Brings and defends civil enforcement actions in the public interest.
- In the DTSA dimension: the federal trade-secret regime is enforced through federal courts, with DOJ criminal authority as its punitive arm (and civil seizure/remedies available to private parties).
- Operates component agencies (FBI for investigations; US Attorneys' offices for district enforcement).
