## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Uphold information rights in the public interest (the ICO's statutory mission).
- Regulate data protection under the UK GDPR / DPA 2018: promote good practice, monitor and enforce compliance.
- Provide authoritative guidance so organizations meet their obligations without needing regulatory intervention.
- Act against misuse of personal data: investigations, enforcement notices, and monetary penalties.

## What the class does

- Publishes guidance, codes of practice, and audits for data protection compliance.
- Handles complaints from data subjects and investigates controllers/processors.
- Issues enforcement notices and fines (up to the UK GDPR's statutory maximums).
- Reports to Parliament on the state of information rights.
