## Definition

The authority or court enforcing trade-secret protection statutes in national and supranational regimes (the Defend Trade Secrets Act in the US, Directive (EU) 2016/943 in member states, the Unfair Competition Prevention Act in Japan). ## Mandate and goals

(Class capacity, expanded from the engaged instances' mandate.)

- Protect lawful controllers of trade secrets against unlawful acquisition, use, and disclosure (the directive's objective).
- Provide effective judicial remedies (injunctions, damages, corrective measures) in every member state.
- Balance protection against fundamental rights and public interest (whistleblower, journalistic, and worker protections in the directive).

## What the class does

- Adjudicate trade-secret misappropriation actions in member-state courts (civil remedies under the directive's Arts 12-15).
- Grant interim and precautionary measures (including injunctions and, where appropriate, seizure of goods).
- Refer interpretation questions to the CJEU (preliminary rulings under Art 267 TFEU).
